# Senior AI Engineer

**Company:** Northwind Analytics
**Location:** Hyderabad, India (Hybrid)
**Team:** Applied AI Platform

## About the role

We are building production LLM systems that sit in front of enterprise knowledge
bases and internal tooling. You will design, build and operate multi-step agentic
workflows that call internal APIs, retrieve from vector stores, and are monitored
in production. This is a hands-on engineering role, not a research role.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of professional software or AI engineering experience
- Strong Python (4+ years), including typed, tested, production-grade code
- Experience building backend services with FastAPI or Flask
- Hands-on experience with AWS or Azure
- Strong understanding of LLM application design (prompting, context management, cost/latency)
- Hands-on RAG experience: chunking, embeddings, retrieval evaluation
- Experience with LangChain
- Experience with LangGraph
- Experience building agentic AI workflows (multi-step, stateful, tool-using)
- Experience with tool / function calling
- Experience deploying and operating AI systems in production
- Experience with Docker and CI/CD

## Preferred

- Vector databases (Qdrant, Pinecone, FAISS, Weaviate, pgvector)
- MLflow or similar experiment/model tracking
- Kubernetes
- Model Context Protocol (MCP)
- Databricks or Snowflake
- LLM observability / evaluation tooling (LangSmith, Langfuse, Ragas)

## What you will do

- Design agent graphs with explicit state, retries and human-in-the-loop checkpoints
- Own retrieval quality: build evaluation sets and measure precision/recall
- Ship services behind FastAPI, containerised, with CI/CD
- Instrument systems for cost, latency and failure analysis
